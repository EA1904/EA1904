> **Research Focus**
>
> My doctoral research investigates **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)**, dynamic relational topologies, and multivariate time-series modeling for **interconnected financial market forecasting**, combining rigorous out-of-sample statistical validation with scalable data engineering.

## Core Research & Engineering Stack

| Domain | Methods & Technologies |
| :--- | :--- |
| **AI & Machine Learning** | Gradient Boosting (XGBoost), Ensemble & Tree Methods, Time-Series Forecasting, Bayesian Optimization, Scikit-learn |
